## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Klaassen 用约一周的晚间时间做了一个环境音乐 DAW，支持 Ableton Push、多路麦克风、效果器和合成器，还能让内置代理参与编曲。他在演示里用它尝试了带利底亚调式的鸟声。

证据与边界　工期和硬件支持为作者自述。原帖有完整操作录屏，可看到他怎样使用这个原型，兼容性与稳定性仍需按具体设备测试。

## Original post text

Fable 5.2 is crazy creative.

I’ve made music my whole life and used to be a film composer. So I vibe coded my dream ambient DAW, at night, in about a week.

Ableton Push support, multiple mics, effects, a synth, and an agent inside that composes with me. I asked for birds in Lydian and it played birds in Lydian.

I feel supported by this model. It augments me, and the whole point is that I play more music.

If you’re a filmmaker or a designer or you use any professional piece of software, go make your own version that is yours.
