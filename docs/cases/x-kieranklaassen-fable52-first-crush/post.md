## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Klaassen 想了解如何酿酒，让模型把清洗罐体、压碎葡萄、用比重计读数、称取添加剂和选酵母做成逐步操作。游戏会在操作时解释每步的缘由。

证据与边界　作者发布了长录屏，也提到动画仍需打磨。游戏中的数值属于其教学场景，酿造实践要以实际工艺条件为准。

## Original post text

I love Fable 5.2 almost as much as I love wine.

We can now make educational games that go deep.

I want to learn how to make wine, so I had it build me a game called First Crush. You scrub the vat, crush 250 kilos of fruit, read the sugar with a hydrometer, weigh out 15 grams of sulfite and pick a yeast, and it explains why as you go.

The story is surprisingly nerdy and I’m not bored. The animations still need work. I think it playtested itself for hours.

Pick a thing you love, make a game about it, and go learn shit.
