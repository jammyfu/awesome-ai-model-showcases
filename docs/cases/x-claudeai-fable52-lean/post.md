## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Anthropic 让 1,000 个运行早期 Fable 5.2 的代理并行复现费马大定理的形式化证明。代理通过共享数据库领任务、传笔记，用 Git 汇总 Lean 证明。官方技术文给出的完成时间是 28.7 小时。

证据与边界　这是对已知定理的机检形式化。团队预先给了 60 条待证陈述、经典路线的简述、相关定义和 47 个已证引理。

## Original post text

A team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalized Fermat’s Last Theorem in 30 hours, translating the proof into a language a computer can check step by step.

With the same setup, Opus 5.5 completed only 26% in 18 hours, and barely made any more progress after 30 hours.
