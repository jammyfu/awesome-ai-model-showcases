## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Anthropic 用早期 Fable 5.2 的代理做了 203 道尚标为开放的混合整数规划题。官方报告 72 道找到更好的解，45 道给出最优性证明，两组有 18 道重叠。

MIPLIB is a library of optimization problems, such as how best to schedule airline crews and delivery trucks.
