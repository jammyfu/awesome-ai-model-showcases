## Original post text

This is actually INSANE. 

Fable 5.2 just created this in one shot, and I did it live on stream. 

I gave Claude one prompt. 

It wrote code that builds the entire room in Blender: the desk, the MacBook, the light, even Clawd.

The laptop screen is a web page Claude built to look like the Claude app, played on the laptop inside the 3D scene.

Then it animated everything itself: 13 camera shots, every single key press, Clawd jumping around, and the sun going from golden hour to night to sunrise.

It rented 10 GPUs on RunPod and rendered the whole thing in 4K in 52 minutes. Total cost: $5.75.

It graded it, added the titles, and made the music and sound effects with ElevenLabs.
