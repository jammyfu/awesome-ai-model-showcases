## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Every 的 Michael Taylor 把研究过程交给代理做成短纪录片。流程先由代理拟采访问题，让本人讲述，再根据录音组织成片。

证据与边界　Every 团队发布串介绍了这项试用，Frontier 项目页提供了方法摘要。片中原始讲述来自研究者本人。

简介　Every 的 Jack Cheng 给模型一张玩具房照片。团队发布的 Fable 5.2 版本把照片里的物件用到玩法中，鞋子成为障碍，水杯成为道具。

证据与边界　这里链接的是 Every 发布时明确标注 Fable 5.2 的团队试用，照片由制作者提供。

## Original post text

BREAKING:

@AnthropicAI just dropped Claude Fable 5.2. It’s a paradigm-shifting model if you have a project ambitious enough to give it.

We’ve been testing it at @every, and I’ve seen this crazy explosion of creativity on the team. People are making their own creative software, games for their kids, and documentaries instead of slide decks.

Here’s your day zero vibe check:

• Coding: @kieranklaassen built Everycut, a video editor with the features we use from Premiere, Descript, and other tools—all in one app, with an agent built in. The first version was live in 3 days, and we immediately used it for our internal show-and-tell. This feels like a new era of professionals building their own professional tools, tailored to exactly how they work.

• Games and design: @jackcheng gave it a photo of his toddler’s messy playroom. It turned the actual toys into a playable racing game, with fun details like a stray shoe as an obstacle and a sippy cup as a power-up.

• Knowledge work: @hammer_mt turned his research into a documentary. The model came up with interview questions, he answered them on camera, and it cut the footage together. He called this the most productive 3 weeks of his career.

• A different way of working: Give it a big job and leave it running overnight or over a weekend. But tell it what “done” means: Mike found it could finish the build, then spend another day or two testing. Set the goal, the budget, and how much checking you need before walking away.

• The catch: It’s slow and burns through tokens. Our early-access usage was free, and these projects would have been expensive at API rates. It’s 20% cheaper than Fable 5.1, but for everyday writing, emails, or quick back-and-forth coding, I’d usually reach for something else.

The clearest signal: When our early access ended, @TylerNishida stopped working on some projects until he could get this model back. He didn’t want to build them with anything else.

The best measure of a new model is whether it makes you try things you wouldn’t have tried before.

Fable 5.2 absolutely does that.
