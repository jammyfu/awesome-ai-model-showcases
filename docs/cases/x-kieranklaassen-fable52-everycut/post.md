简介　Kieran Klaassen 给 Every 团队做了一个内部视频编辑器。它把录屏、时间线、可按字删改的转录稿和混音器放在一起，也让内置代理操作同一套按钮。音频和代理引擎复用了他先前做的环境音乐工作站。

证据与边界　作者称第一版 3 天上线，团队后来在里面开过会。公开素材是团队演示，功能范围以演示和作者说明为准。

## Original post text

I told the team at @every that with Fable 5.2, one person can build professional software. Nobody believed me.

Three days later a video editor was live for the team. It’s called Everycut. It has screen recording, a timeline, a transcript you cut by its words, a mixer, and an agent that presses the same buttons I press.

I pitched it as Descript, Screen Studio, After Effects and Premiere in one. A week in, we held a team meeting in it.

The first time I used it for real, I typed one sentence into Claude and it made the video.

The audio and agent engines came from my ambient DAW.

Whatever we can dream can now be built, so we should dream bigger.
