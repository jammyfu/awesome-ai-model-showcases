## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Every 的 Baynkr 把相同设计任务分配给 Fable 5.2 的不同推理档位，再盲评输出。团队观察到 High 版在静态设计上更合意，Max 有时让排版更繁复。

证据与边界　这组判断来自团队的设计试用。团队把静态布局和动效交互分开讨论，两类任务的结果不能互相代替。

## Original post text

Turning Fable 5.2’s effort setting all the way up often made its design work worse.

@baynkr tested the same tasks at different settings and rated the results blind. For design taste, high beat max.

Our team’s starting points:

• Code: low
• Research and writing: medium
• Design: high
• Animation and interaction details: max

That last distinction matters. A higher setting could improve motion and interactions while making a static layout too busy.
