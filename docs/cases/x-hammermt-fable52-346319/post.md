## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　Michael Taylor 看电影时让 Fable 5.2 做三维战场地图。模型按场景排列事件，并估算画面外人物在同一时刻的位置。

证据与边界　作者发了从霍博肯方向查看战场的录屏。画外人物的位置是推断，精确度没有逐镜核验。

## Original post text

On Friday I was watching the Avengers with my kids (their first time) and got Fable 5.2 access back, so I made a 3D model of the battle of New York. It worked over the weekend and matched it scene for scene to the movie estimating where offscreen characters would be.
