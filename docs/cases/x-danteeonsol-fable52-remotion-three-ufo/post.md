## Wiki summary (paraphrase, not the original post)

简介　作者只给了一句场景需求。Fable 5.2 用 Three.js 和 Remotion 组织了约 30 秒的成片，包含五个镜头。

证据与边界　作者称原版为 4K、30 fps，模型、纹理和声音都由代码生成。可从完整短片核对五个镜头的组织。

## Original post text

Benchmarked Claude Fable 5.2 with one sentence:

"make me a 30 second video of a UFO visiting Area 51 and greeting humans, ultra detailed, cinematic lighting"

It wrote the whole thing in three.js + Remotion. No imported models, no textures, no stock audio. Every pixel and every sound is code.

4K, 30 fps, 5 shots. 👇
